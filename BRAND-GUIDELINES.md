## 1. The Brand Name

```
AS ♭ MS
```

> The brand name is always written as **AS ♭ MS** — two letters, the Repair Note symbol in the centre, two letters. Never written as "AS&MS", "AS and MS", or "A.S. & M.S."

The full business name **Abhishek Stores and Mobile Shop** is used only in legal, SEO metadata, and footer small-print. In all visual contexts — logo, signage, website header, social profiles, visiting cards — the brand is **AS ♭ MS**.

## 2. The Symbol — "The Repair Note" ( ♭ )

### 2.1 What It Is

The centrepiece of the brand identity is a **custom symbol** that takes the shape of a **musical flat sign (♭)** but is redrawn with deliberate meaning as a **repair mark**. It replaces the conventional "&" ampersand between AS and MS.

The flat sign (♭) is chosen because:
- It is a **genuine musical notation symbol** — aesthetic, elegant, universally recognised
- Its form is a **vertical stroke + a curved lower loop** — which mirrors the silhouette of a **precision screwdriver**: the stem is the handle, the loop is the working tip curling around a screw head
- It reads as **a connector** between two things (AS and MS) — just as a repair connects a broken device back to working life
- It is **simple enough to tattoo, embroider, or etch** — meaning it works at any size, any medium

### 2.2 Symbol Name

**"The Repair Note"**

Internal name used across all brand documents, files, and conversations when referring to the ♭ symbol.

### 2.3 Symbolic Meaning — Layered

| Layer | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| **Musical** | A flat note (♭) — something restored to its pure, intended state. In music, a flat corrects pitch. In repair, we correct damage. |
| **Tool** | The vertical stem is a screwdriver handle. The lower bowl is the head gripping a screw. Together: precision craftsmanship. |
| **Flow** | The curve of the loop suggests a circuit trace — the language of electronics, the substrate of every device we fix. |
| **Bridge** | Sitting between AS and MS, it bridges the two halves of the name — just as the shop bridges you and your working device. |
| **Simplicity** | One stroke. One curve. No ornamentation. Repair is direct — diagnose, fix, return. The symbol is the same. |

### 2.4 Symbol Anatomy

```
     │  ← Vertical stroke (stem)
     │     — represents the technician's precision,
     │       the straightness of purpose
     │
    ╭─╮ ← Lower bowl / loop
    ╰─╯   — represents the screw being turned,
            the circuit completing,
            the device returning to full function
```

The stroke-to-bowl ratio is **3:1** (stem is 3× the height of the bowl). This ratio is non-negotiable — it is what makes it read as both a musical note and a screwdriver silhouette simultaneously.

### 2.5 Symbol as Custom SVG Mark

When rendered in the logo, the ♭ is **not** the Unicode flat character. It is a custom-drawn vector path with these properties:

- **Stroke weight:** Monoline (uniform stroke width throughout)
- **Stem:** Perfectly vertical, no taper
- **Bowl:** D-shaped loop, bowl opening faces right
- **Corner where stem meets bowl:** Smooth bezier curve, not a sharp join
- **Overall feel:** Confident, clean, single-stroke calligraphic
- **No serifs. No fills. Outline only (or solid fill — see colour section)**

### 2.6 Symbol Colour Variations

| Variant | Background | Symbol Colour | Use When |
|---------|-----------|--------------|---------|
| **Primary** | White / Off-white | Electric Blue `#1A6CF2` | Website header, digital primary |
| **Reversed** | Electric Blue | White `#FFFFFF` | Dark backgrounds, app icons |
| **Accent** | Dark `#0F172A` | Saffron `#F5820A` | Promotional banners, social media |
| **Mono Black** | White | Pure Black `#000000` | Print, legal documents |
| **Mono White** | Black | Pure White `#FFFFFF` | Dark print, embossing |
| **Emboss / Stamp** | None | Debossed / foiled | Visiting cards, packaging stickers |

### 2.7 Symbol Minimum Size

| Medium | Minimum Size |
|--------|-------------|
| Digital (screen) | 16px height |
| Print | 6mm height |
| Visiting card (standalone mark) | 8mm height |
| Signage | 40mm height |

Never display the symbol below these sizes. The bowl loop must be clearly legible at minimum size.

### 2.8 Clear Space Rule

The symbol always has clear space on all four sides equal to **the height of the bowl loop**. No other element, text, or edge may enter this zone.

```
  ↕ [bowl height]
← [bowl height] → ♭ ← [bowl height] →
  ↕ [bowl height]
```

### 2.9 What You Must Never Do

- Never rotate the symbol
- Never stretch or distort it
- Never add a drop shadow
- Never use a gradient fill on the symbol itself
- Never use the Unicode ♭ character as a substitute in visual contexts (it has serif features that differ from the custom mark)
- Never place it on a busy photographic background without a clear backing
- Never use it smaller than minimum sizes
- Never change its proportions (stem-to-bowl ratio must remain 3:1)

## 3. Logo Construction

### 3.1 Full Wordmark

```
AS  ♭  MS
```

- **Typeface:** Poppins SemiBold (weight 600) for AS and MS
- **Symbol:** Custom Repair Note mark, same cap-height as the letters
- **Spacing:** Equal spacing between AS and ♭, and between ♭ and MS — each gap = 0.5× the width of the letter "A"
- **Baseline:** All three elements share a common baseline
- **Letter-spacing for AS and MS:** 0.05em (very slight tracking for air)

### 3.2 Stacked Version (for square applications)

```
  ♭
AS MS
```

Used for: app icons, profile pictures, favicon, sticker, stamp.

### 3.3 Compact Mark (symbol only)

Just ♭ alone — used for favicon (16×16, 32×32), app icon, WhatsApp profile picture.

## 7. Imagery Direction

### 7.1 Photography Style

- **Real photos over stock** — actual shop, actual technician, actual devices
- **Warm-toned natural light** — not cold studio lighting
- **Action shots** — technician's hands at work, screwdriver in hand, screen lit up
- **Human presence** — a hand, an arm, a face (partial) to humanise the brand
- **Clean backgrounds** — device on a white or off-white surface, no clutter

### 7.2 What to Avoid

- Cold blue-tinted stock photography
- Floating 3D devices with no context
- Cartoon illustrations (unless used as small service icons)
- Dark, dramatic photography (leave that to premium brands like Fixkart — AS & MS should feel warm and local)

### 7.3 Image Aspect Ratios

| Use | Ratio |
|-----|-------|
| Hero | 16:9 |
| Service cards | 4:3 |
| Gallery | Mix of 1:1 and 4:3 |
| OG image | 1200×630px (exactly) |
| Favicon | 512×512px (square) |

## 8. Voice & Tone

| Attribute | What It Means |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Direct** | No waffle. "We come to you" not "Our trained professionals are available for doorstep visits" |
| **Warm** | Friendly, local. Like a trusted neighbour. Not corporate. |
| **Confident** | "We fix it or you don't pay." Not "We try our best." |
| **Simple** | Short sentences. One idea per line. Easy to scan. |
| **Local** | Reference areas — E City, Hosa Road, South Bengaluru. Not generic "Bangalore". |

### 8.1 Tone Examples

| Avoid | Use Instead |
|-------|-------------|
| "We offer comprehensive mobile device repair solutions" | "Your phone fixed. At your door." |
| "Our technicians are available for doorstep visits" | "We come to you." |
| "We strive to provide quality service" | "Repaired right, or you don't pay." |
| "Serving the Bangalore metropolitan area" | "Serving E City, Hosa Road & all of South Bengaluru" |
